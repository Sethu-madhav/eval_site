Instructions for labeling

Real Photo:
A real picture taken with a camera, though small edits like adding labels are okay. but the primary content must remain a real image without significant alterations.
Edited Image:
A photograph that has undergone human made digital edits many times or digital work made by humans like (graphs, tables, artwork).
AI Generated:
An image created entirely by artificial intelligence, not captured by a camera (from models like Midjourney or Stable Diffusion).
